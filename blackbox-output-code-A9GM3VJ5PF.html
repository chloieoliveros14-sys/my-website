Open Me
With sincerity and hope, Margaux

Hello, :)

You've probably opened this letter and wondered why I sent it, right? Well, I've decided to express myself in a unique way, and for my case, this is how I thought of doing it.

Okay, so first off, I'd like to tell you that I liked you since 8th grade, and honestly, I didn't really have the guts to tell you back then. Because I sensed this feeling that it wouldn't work out and I would just end up losing the friendship we already had. And like, we already weren't that close, so to sum it up, it was a really big risk to take that time. And I chose to not risk it. And knowing myself, I wasn't really the type of person to expect something more, although I continued to admire you that time, and it was fun.

Time passed so quickly, and before I knew it, I had moved to another city. That change really shaped my life in many ways. Still, every now and then, I find myself thinking about the people I left behind, including you, and I remember both the good and the difficult moments I experienced there.

To be honest, my feelings toward you weren't always steady. It wasn't a constant affection at first—it came and went, on and off—but it was real in its own way. Only because I really couldn't reach you and I thought you forgot about me. Because, even before, like in personal settings, we never really got the chance to talk lololol. So I was like trying to play it cool again.

Also, you remind me of my favorite songs so much, like, I swear, if I could describe you and picture how you look, you'd look like my favorite song WAHAHAHAHA.

And then one day I saw you again. I was really happy and excited, but I just couldn't reach you again for some reason. I feel awkward kasi WHAAHHAHAH, but it started off as a happy crush. But, this past week I feel like ang duwag ko naman if I didn't confess pa because honestly this school year is about to end, and I want to live it to the fullest. Even if I get rejected, I'll know I was brave enough to follow my heart. At least I was brave enough to tell someone the quiet admiration and appreciation I truly carry for them.

- from, Margaux
